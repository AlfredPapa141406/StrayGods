Frequently Asked Questions

What are the requirements to join?

We welcome dedicated players who are active, friendly, and willing to contribute to our community. Specific level and class requirements may vary.

How long does the application process take?

Applications are typically reviewed within 48-72 hours. You'll receive a response through Discord or email.

What activities does the guild participate in?

We organize regular WoE events, guild dungeons, social gatherings, and collaborative activities for all members.
